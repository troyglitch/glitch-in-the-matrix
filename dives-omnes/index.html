What This Actually Is in plain words — for once

we built autonomous agents that execute specific commercial outcomes — growth, lead generation, brand operations, cfo-grade decision work. each agent is wrapped as a play: a contracted outcome with a measurement window and a success threshold. you engage the play. the play executes. the system measures the result. you pay only if it hits the threshold.

we don't sell tools. we don't sell time. we sell the outcome.

ENGAGE

Engage a Play

you have a brand — or you want one. you pick a play with a known outcome contract: deliver this metric, by this date, against this baseline. the play runs. we take a performance cut only if it hits.

BUILD

Build a Play

you have engineering or product chops. compose your own play from our pills and agents. list it in the marketplace. earn a share of every engagement. we take a marketplace cut.

The cut is performance-based. nobody gets paid up front. the exact percentage is intentionally redacted on folio i — it goes on the ledger when the first play closes. we will be lower than what you'd guess.

The plays are not crypto. not stocks. not prediction markets. they are autonomous-agent executions against real commerce work — growth, lead gen, brand ops — productized as outcome contracts. each one has a measurement contract, a success threshold, and a settlement engine. real work. real metric. real receipt.

The risk is real. plays can miss thresholds. then nobody gets paid — not the play-builder, not us. that's the contract. it forces us to only ship plays we believe will hit.
